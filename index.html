Drive Sentry

Assistive only. The driver stays responsible.

Drive Sentry is a driving aid, not a replacement for your attention. It can miss lanes, vehicles, and hazards, and it can warn you when nothing is wrong.

Set it up while parked. Mount your phone horizontally (landscape) and level, where it never blocks your view of the road.

Drive Sentry is off

You are not being warned.

The camera and alerts are stopped. Tap Turn on when you’re ready to drive.

Ready to drive

For full screen and a locked landscape view, install Drive Sentry first. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app, and open it from your home screen.
